# Code of conduct

wellhead follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone acts on
behalf of the project.

## Report an incident

Report privately to the [maintainers](MAINTAINERS.md) through GitHub's private reporting form
([direct link](https://github.com/wellhead-dev/wellhead/security/advisories/new)) and start the title
with `Conduct:`. Only the maintainers can read it, and a conduct report is never published as an
advisory. A maintainer answers within three business days. A maintainer who is the subject of a report,
or close to it, takes no part in handling it.

You can also report content on GitHub to GitHub itself with **Report content**.

## What responders do

Acknowledge the report, keep the reporter's identity confidential, look at the evidence, act on
interim risk, choose a remedy that fits (a warning, removing content, a temporary or permanent ban),
and tell the reporter the outcome.
